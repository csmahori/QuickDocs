Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026 Effective from the date of publication

1. Introduction

Welcome to EasyDocuments.online ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide an online marriage biodata maker that lets you create, preview, and download a professional biodata PDF. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our service, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly (in the biodata form):

  • Personal details: name, date of birth, height, education, occupation, income range
  • Family details: parents' names and occupations, siblings
  • Contact details: state, city, mobile number
  • Religion, community / caste, gotra / rashi (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional — processed locally in your browser)
Important: All biodata form data is processed locally in your browser and is not stored on our servers. We do not maintain a database of your personal biodata information.

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type, device type, and operating system (via Cloudflare analytics)
  • Pages visited and time spent (aggregate, anonymised)
  • IP address (processed by Cloudflare, not stored by us directly)

Payment information: Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not collect or store your card number, UPI ID, or banking credentials. Razorpay's privacy policy governs the handling of your payment data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and deliver your biodata PDF
  • To process and verify your payment via Razorpay
  • To respond to support requests submitted via WhatsApp or our feedback form
  • To analyse aggregate website usage and improve our service
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use the browser's localStorage to remember your offer/discount preferences during a session. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. Third-party scripts on our site (Cloudflare, Razorpay checkout) may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Razorpay — Payment processing. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — CDN, DDoS protection, and web analytics. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Web3Forms — Feature request / feedback form submissions. Web3Forms Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — Font loading for the web interface.

6. Data Retention

Since biodata form data is processed entirely in your browser, we do not retain it after your session. Payment transaction records are retained by Razorpay as required by applicable law. Feedback or support messages submitted via our form are retained only as long as needed to respond to your query.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect our website infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you not to share sensitive personal information beyond what is required to create your biodata.

8. Your Rights

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data of yours we process
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request erasure of your personal data (where applicable)
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. Since biodata form data is not stored on our servers, erasure requests for that data are automatically fulfilled when you close your browser tab.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided personal data to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove such information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, please reach out to us:

  • Website: easydocuments.online

  • Feature / Feedback Form: Available in the Request section of our homepage
