    What is a Marriage Biodata?

    A marriage biodata is a one-page document used in Indian matrimonial processes to introduce a prospective bride or groom to families. It typically includes personal details (name, date of birth, height, complexion), family background (parents' names, siblings, community, gotra), education and career, and contact information. A well-designed biodata makes a strong first impression and is shared via WhatsApp, matrimonial apps, or family networks.

    Why Use EasyDocuments to Make Your Biodata?

    How to Write a Marriage Biodata — Tips

    • Keep it to one page — families prefer concise, scannable biodatas
    • Include a clear photo — a recent, well-lit photo builds trust
    • Mention gotra and community — important for matching in Hindu matrimonial customs
    • Be accurate about height and complexion — honesty avoids awkward first meetings
    • Add a WhatsApp number — fastest way for families to connect
    • Choose Hindi or English — match the preference of families you're sharing with
